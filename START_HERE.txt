❓ COMMON QUESTIONS

Q: Why is it green?
A: Green = using LIVE sensor data (accurate!)
   Blue = using historical data (fallback)

Q: Why is the timestamp 5 minutes in future?
A: That's when the prediction is for (5-min horizon)

Q: Is my app broken?
A: No! It's improved. Green chip = working perfectly!

Q: What if I see blue instead of green?
A: Database might not have recent data. Check:
   python check_live_sensor_tables.py

Q: Can I change the 5 minute prediction window?
A: Yes! Modify request to: {"horizon_minutes": 15}
